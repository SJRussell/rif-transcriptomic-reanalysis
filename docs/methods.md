# Complete analytical methods

This document describes the frozen study and points to the implementing scripts. Cohort inclusion and sample identity are taken from source metadata and recorded manifests, never inferred from expression. No classifier is retrained in this repository.

## 1. Review, eligibility and cohort roles

The systematic review is guided by PRISMA 2020. The review flow is 461 identified records, 344 after deduplication, 298 after exclusion of 46 nonhuman reports, 120 after exclusion of 178 reports without RIF, 30 sought for data after 90 other eligibility exclusions, and 14 included after 16 data-unavailable exclusions. One of the 14 cohorts, GSE223672, has no within-study control group, leaving 13 primary contrasts. The full database search strategies and some record-level screening details are not documented in this repository. The source studies' comparator definitions are retained rather than claiming that all controls have documented live births.

Discovery, broader external evaluation, technical comparisons and contextual/unavailable datasets have separate roles. GSE248784 and GSE207362 are broader, publication-linked external evaluations. GSE243550 is a repository-labelled technical comparison. GSE307510 uses five native biopsies, excluding matched chip samples. Xu/PRJNA1335587 remains a technical evaluation with unresolved publication/deposit discrepancies. None enters discovery selection or weighting. See `cohorts.md` for each cohort's role, sample decisions and the datasets considered and not used.

## 2. Source preparation and identities

The exact source artifacts, sizes and SHA-256 hashes are in `provenance/inputs.csv`. The 242 raw GSE58144 and GSE71331 array files are identified by accession. Source count matrices must contain nonnegative integer counts; microarray matrices must retain their recorded log-expression scale. The discovery metadata supplies the fixed sample list and ordering. No new exclusions are selected during repository preparation.

GSE58144 is rebuilt from 230 dye-swap arrays. The recorded sample/reference channel orientation is applied, foreground intensities receive normexp background correction (offset 0), within-array loess normalizes log ratios, and paired arrays are averaged for each donor. The GEO deposited expression is an orientation check, not an alternative clinical label. Probes map through GPL15789; ambiguous mappings are removed. The 113-donor primary set (42 RIF, 71 controls) follows the two original PCA exclusions; all 115 donors are also tested. Batch and recorded biopsy day enter the differential-expression model. Case-blind batch removal is used only for the classifier input. See `subtypes/rebuild_gse58144.R` and the orientation notes in `docs/sources/`.

GSE71331 is read from raw Agilent files, with normexp offset 0 and between-array quantile normalization. Annotation ProbeID is joined to the platform feature ID and then to ProbeName, not to the internal ProbeUID. Control features and ambiguous symbol mappings are excluded. No additional log transform is applied. See `baseline/fit_studies.R`.

PRJNA576073 uses the six existing aligned BAMs recounted with featureCounts 2.0.3: `-t exon -g gene_id -s 0 -p --countReadPairs`, four threads. Reverse-strand `-s 2` counts are a sensitivity. The annotation is the recorded `T2T.gtf`, identified by checksum; its assembly/provider is not guessed. Upstream alignment parameters cannot be reconstructed from a recount command. See `preparation/recount_prjna.py` and the manifest.

Xu/PRJNA1335587 reads are processed from checksum-verified ENA FASTQs with Cutadapt, FastQC and STAR against GRCh38 and GENCODE v49, and counted in forward, reverse and unstranded modes (`subtypes/nibi_xu_pipeline.py`). The count direction follows a rule fixed on 8 September 2026, before any aligned expression was inspected: forward if every library has at least 0.80 of its assigned reads on the forward strand, reverse if every library has at most 0.20, otherwise unstranded. Libraries with fewer than one million retained reads or under 50% unique alignment are flagged for inspection and not excluded. There is no outcome-based outlier removal. Modelling follows section 3, with age adjustment as a sensitivity. See `docs/source_checks/Xu2026/RAW_VALIDATION_RULES.md`.

Other discovery microarray/count inputs are the exact archived matrices. The original preprocessing notebooks in `analysis/as_run/historical_preprocessing/` document their provenance; the limits of that record are listed in the README. In particular, the discovery single-cell arms use existing donor pseudobulk counts, not new cell annotations or reselected cells.

## 3. Gene harmonization and within-study models

The frozen HGNC table is filtered to approved symbols. Exact approved symbols take precedence, followed by unambiguous previous symbols, aliases, Ensembl identifiers and Entrez identifiers in the original recorded order. Ensembl version suffixes are removed. Ambiguous/unmapped identifiers and nonfinite expression rows are excluded. Rows mapping to one symbol are summed for counts and averaged for arrays. The mapping audit records retained and rejected rows; an annotation row is not necessarily one biological gene.

Each study is modelled separately with control as reference and RIF as the tested coefficient. Microarray genes with zero variance are removed; remaining genes use limma and robust empirical Bayes moderation. Count data use edgeR `filterByExpr` with the full design, TMM normalization, limma-voom and robust empirical Bayes moderation. GSE58144 includes batch and biopsy day, and Bui includes recorded LH-relative biopsy day centred at 7. Other primary contrasts use the fixed group-only design. Rank, replicate-count, count-integrality and sample-match assertions are retained. Positive log2 fold change and signed Z mean higher expression in RIF.

The moderated t test yields a signed normal Z using its two-sided P value; log-tail calculations avoid underflow. The model writer exports fold change, moderated standard error, t, degrees of freedom, P, Z, BH FDR, tested-gene coverage and the expression/design object. The functions are in `baseline/fit_studies.R`. The twelve non-GSE58144 contrasts were fitted by running its study loop on those twelve studies (see `analysis/README.md`). GSE58144 is fitted by `subtypes/rebuild_gse58144.R`.

## 4. Study-level synthesis

For study s, effective sample size is `4*n_RIF*n_control/(n_RIF+n_control)` and its Z weight is the square root of that quantity. For each gene, pooled Z is `sum(w_s*Z_s)/sqrt(sum(w_s^2))` over studies with a tested estimate. A missing study estimate is absent, not a zero effect. Primary genes must be tested in at least two microarray and two RNA-seq studies: 16,008 genes. The all-13-study intersection is 11,349. P values are two-sided normal tails; BH adjustment is across the stated testing family. Platform-specific Z and BH FDR use the same primary gene family. The weighted descriptive fold change is not a common fixed-effect estimate.

Influence analyses keep the primary gene family fixed while omitting individual studies, all pseudobulk studies, or platform subsets; unestimable omission P values become 1. Other sensitivities include total/equal weights, all 115 GSE58144 donors, alternative timing/composition sets, Bui without timing adjustment, reverse-strand counts, RNA quality weights and the historical two-stage weighting. The all-sample Bui and GSE58144 sensitivities update their sample-size weights. Random-effects fold changes, Q, I2 and DL tau2 are descriptive; modified Hartung-Knapp inference floors its scale at 1. See `pool_studies.R` and `final_sensitivities.R`.

## 5. Pathways and fixed programs

FGSEA uses the frozen HGNC-mapped gene sets and signed Z ranks: set size 15–500, `nPermSimple=10000`, `eps=1e-10`, serial execution and seed 20260908. Pooled, platform-specific, GSE111974-omitted and pseudobulk-omitted ranks are analyzed separately. Immune/interferon directions disagree between the array and RNA-seq arms and must not be presented as a uniform effect.

The 57 fixed programs are 50 Hallmarks and seven explicitly listed panels: ciliary motility, NK cytotoxicity, myeloid lineage, T-cell lineage, epithelial lineage, stromal lineage and decidualization. Membership is in the source code and the frozen reference object `fixed_programs.rds` (listed in `provenance/inputs.csv`); source citations are in `docs/sources/fixed_marker_program_sources.csv`. Sets need at least five tested genes. FRY tests coordinated change within a set; CAMERA tests competitive enrichment, with `inter.gene.cor=NA`. Per-study signed FRY Z statistics use the same effective-size pooling and BH across 57 programs. Gene-standardized mean scores describe relative program expression; they are not estimated cell proportions.

Cao composition uses the published donor fractions, a centred log-ratio transform with pseudocount 1e-4, the fixed LH+7, PGT-inclusive and mixed-time comparisons, and recorded exact/Monte Carlo label permutations. The first two composition PCs enter the specified sensitivity model. Seven exploratory program slopes (six marker panels and the apical junction Hallmark) use the twelve ordinary control donors and recorded LH day. See `composition_analysis.R`, `timing_programs.R`, `pathways_and_programs.R` and `program_replication.R`.

## 6. Frozen discovery scores and external replication

In each leave-one-study-out evaluation, the panel is selected using only the remaining discovery studies. A gene must be tested in at least two array and two RNA-seq training studies and have the same sign of pooled Z in the array arm and in the RNA-seq arm. Eligible genes are ranked by absolute pooled Z (section 4 weights), and the top 25 positive and top 25 negative genes form the panel: 25 genes higher and 25 lower in RIF. The independent external panel is selected on all discovery cohorts and then frozen. The score contrasts within-sample gene percentile ranks in the two directions. Panel membership is exported; absent genes are recorded, not silently replaced. Published Altmäe and Chettiar panels are fixed benchmarks; the receptivity direction is explicitly reversed once where required by the stated hypothesis, never flipped after seeing the AUC.

AUC uses the original score orientation. Confidence intervals use 2,000 stratified donor bootstraps, seed 20260908, and are omitted for fewer than five donors per group or degenerate intervals under the recorded rule. Directional gene replication tests the frozen discovery direction with one-sided normal-tail P values and BH over testable discovery DEGs. Program replication analogously uses the frozen discovery direction and the stated program family; two-sided and directional FDR are separate. See `validate_signatures.R`, `program_replication.R` and `evaluate_new_cohorts.R`.

GSE207362 uses the exact Fukui 12+26 published subset mapped to column suffixes. This assumes the publication's within-group indices identify those suffixes; the deposit has no independent donor key. It is external because biopsies were HRT P+7. The NA-containing ANK2 row is dropped, no library is dropped, and the discovery HGNC/model code is reused without covariates. The cohort was fitted on the Digital Research Alliance of Canada Nibi cluster: R 4.4.3 (limma 3.62.1, edgeR 4.4.0) for the fit, scores and replication, and R 4.5.0 (caret 7.0-1, randomForest 4.7-1.2, nnet 7.3-20) for MetaRIF. That run is the recorded reference. A repeat in R 4.6.1 (limma 3.68.4, edgeR 4.10.1) from an independent download of the same GEO file gave identical AUCs, intervals, program results and MetaRIF assignments. It put 615 rather than 616 genes in the discovery direction and 1,342 rather than 1,351 at nominal p < 0.05, which reflects the limma version. Scripts are in `analysis/as_run/gse207362/`; session records are in `environments/observed/`.

## 7. Released MetaRIF models and specificity

The helper and model artifact are pinned to commit `dca97d587b9ba437f308aa2f15086e6f294d101d`. No fitting, tuning or clinical relabelling occurs. The released two-pass gene-wise scaling and probability assignment rules are preserved. With p1 and p2 the class probabilities of the two models, a sample is RIF1 (RIF-I, immune) if p1 > 0.5 and p1 > p2 + 0.1, RIF2 (RIF-M, metabolic) if p2 > 0.5 and p2 > p1 + 0.1, Normal if both are below 0.5, and Uncertain otherwise. Output files use the labels RIF1 and RIF2. Symbols map to Entrez IDs; the two models use 64 and 19 features, with a 90% coverage eligibility rule and explicit coverage reports. Missing/nonfinite model features follow the released zero-after-scaling handling. The wrapper checks agreement with the released helpers.

Arrays use source log expression (with the specified case-blind GSE58144 adjustment); RNA-seq uses unlogged CPM, with log-CPM sensitivity. A fixed positive multiplicative gene-length factor cancels under gene-wise standardization, explaining CPM/FPKM equivalence for a fixed length per gene. This invariance does **not** make different array normalization methods or log transformations generally interchangeable. Feature coverage, helper equivalence, cohort-context sensitivity and the observed preprocessing departures remain explicit.

Published fitting/model-selection cohorts are distinguished from the ten other discovery cohorts (69 RIF, 57 controls). Clinical enrichment uses cohort-stratified Mantel-Haenszel odds ratios with the original output categories, including Uncertain. Fixed immune/metabolic axes, mixture comparisons and subtype-selected program contrasts are exploratory. Programs are also evaluated after removing classifier genes. Strong within-selected-subtype expression differences are not independent proof of clinical specificity. See `evaluate_metarif.R`, `analyze_heterogeneity.R`, `state_specificity.R` and `clinical_specificity_sensitivities.R`.

## 8. External donor-lineage analysis

GSE248784 GEO title order determines donor labels, including two internal filename/title disagreements. The 21 matrices share 32,058 Ensembl features. Cells meet `200 <= detected genes <= 7000`, UMI `<40000` and mitochondrial UMI fraction `<0.30`; mitochondrial `<0.10` is a sensitivity. Donor pseudobulk sums retained counts. No recreation of unavailable author cell annotations or doublet labels is claimed.

The fixed broad marker panels operate without clinical labels. Primary assignment needs mean log1p normalized panel score >=0.25, at least two detected panel genes, and a >=0.10 gap to the next panel, otherwise Unassigned. The documented T/NK override is preserved. Strict annotation needs at least three genes, gap >=0.25, mitochondrial fraction <0.10 and no T/NK override. At least 50 assigned cells per donor/lineage and at least five donors per group are required. The inferential unit is the donor, not the cell. Classifier and annotation-panel genes are excluded from lineage program tests. Gene FDR is within lineage; program FDR is global across eligible lineage/program tests within annotation variant. Primary and 17-donor shared-reference analyses find no significant program; the strict annotation has four downward programs. Composition uses donor fractions and donor-level Wilcoxon tests with BH. See `annotate_external_cells.py`, `analyze_celltypes.R`, `celltype_shared_reference.R`.

## 9. Reporting and verification

Reference results are in `results/reference/`: rounded main tables and full-precision supplement extracts. `analysis/verify_pooled_results.py` recomputes signed Z, pooled statistics, BH adjustment, pooled programs, model denominators and Mantel-Haenszel odds ratios from the per-study outputs, and checks the lineage and sample-mapping claims. It needs the full results package, which is not in this repository, and it does not redo raw processing. It passes against the package behind the reference tables: 13 studies, 299 samples, 16,008 genes, 1,840 DEGs, 57 programs, maximum pooled-Z error 6.7e-15. No end-to-end rerun of the models was done when this repository was assembled.
